🫙 Swear Jar Settings

General

Points are severity-weighted: mild words = 1pt, medium = 2pts, strong = 4pts.

Voice capture (meetings)

⚠️ This is different from the rest of the jar. Chrome's built-in speech recognition sends your microphone audio to Google's servers to turn it into text — it is not processed on-device, unlike the page-text scanner. Only your own mic is captured (not other participants), only on meet.google.com / zoom.us tabs, and only while this is turned on. A small "🎙️ Swear Jar listening" badge appears in the corner of the tab whenever it's active.
